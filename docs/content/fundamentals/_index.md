Every humus service, whatever it serves, runs the same App. These pages cover
what that App does, in the order a service meets it: the lifecycle that
builds and runs it, how its settings get their values, the OpenTelemetry
providers it hands to `Init`, the health Service that owns its readiness, and
how to run the whole App inside a test.
