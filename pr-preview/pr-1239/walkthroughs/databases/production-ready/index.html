Production-ready

The checklist the databases controller meets, and how it meets each item, with the page that builds it. Check your own Kubernetes controller against it.

The controller meets each item. Check your own controller against them:

ItemHowWhere
Every setting sourced and checked; a bad one exits 78, naming its variableconfig.Struct, kube’s checks and the controller’s ownThe settings
No secret in the environment, the logs or the spansthe admin DSN, the service account’s token and CA, and the probes’ key are files read through s.Host.FS. A role’s password reaches PostgreSQL as a SCRAM verifier, and a statement’s span carries its summary, never its SQL. A test of the App checks that no password, DSN or token is in a span, a record or a metricThe secrets, as files, Keeping the password out of the SQL, Keeping credentials out of the spans
Certificates renewed without a restartthe probes’ certificate is served through GetCertificate, by a small helper of the walkthrough’s own that reads its file again when it changes. The token’s file is read each time the Client asks, so a rotated one is picked upThe probes, The Connection
Available through a lost replicatwo replicas share the Lease, and the standby takes over when the leader stops. A rollout starts a new replica before it stops an old one, and a PodDisruptionBudget of minAvailable: 1The Settings and the Lease, The Deployment, The disruption budget
Ready only once its caches have syncedSynced as readiness, on an HTTPS probes portThe probes
A rollout loses nothingSIGTERM finishes the reconciles in flight and releases the Lease, in a grace period of 20s, and terminationGracePeriodSeconds is 30. The next leader lists every Database againThe grace period, main
Reconciling again is harmlessthe Secret is the password’s source of truth, so every reconcile sets the same one. The role and the database are ensured, and the drops are IF EXISTSThe password, Provisioning, Dropping
Deleting cleans up, as the Database saysthe finalizer holds a deleted Database until Finalize has dropped what its deletionPolicy says to. The Secret is deleted with it, as the Database controls itThe finalizer, The Secret
Least privilegea ClusterRole that watches and patches Databases and applies Secrets, and a Role for its own Lease and no other. A non-root container, on a read-only root filesystem, with every capability droppedRBAC, The Deployment
Observabletraces, logs and metrics over OTLP, and rules for failing, panicking and stuck reconciles, a backlog and restarts, on humus’s own instruments, and a dashboardObserving
Failures say whya failed step sets Ready to False, with a reason naming the step, PostgresUnavailable or SecretApplyFailedThe conditions
Failures are rehearsedthe failure injection casesThe failure injection cases
Safe by defaultdeletionPolicy defaults to Retain, and name is immutable. No namespace’s scenarios are trustedThe resource and its rules, Trusting a namespace’s scenarios
